Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom song commission cost? +

Commissions start at £70 for The Signature (a 3-minute custom song with digital cover art), £120 for The Cinematic (a 5-minute song with picture slide video), and £189+ for The Legacy (a full luxury production with two songs and a premium music video).

How long does it take to receive a custom song? +

Turnaround depends on current commission volume and package. Message us on WhatsApp with your occasion and date and we'll confirm a delivery timeline before you pay.

Is this an AI-generated song? +

Every Veloréva commission is hand art-directed by our creative team. We use professional production tools, but the lyric writing, arrangement, and creative direction are led by people, not left to an automated template.

How do I start a commission? +

Message us via WhatsApp or complete the Bespoke Enquiry form on this page with your milestones, memories, and style preferences, then make payment to begin composition.
